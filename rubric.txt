Introduction
The scheme is designed to be used for assessing research reports submitted by Honours students in Computer
Science and Big Data Analytics. Projects shall be classified, based on the judgment of the project supervisor, into
the following categories:
• experimental computer science;
• theoretical computer science (TCS).
• philosophy of computation and artificial intelligence (PCAI).
The assessment criteria for the projects of the three types overlap, but differ. When assessing the project, only
the rubrics relevant to the project type should be applied. Usually, the following areas are considered part of TCS:
• analysis of algorithms;
• computability and complexity theory;
• formal methods in computer science;
• mathematical structures in computer science.
